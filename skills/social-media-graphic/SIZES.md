# Social media sizes

Pixel sizes for static graphics. Platforms change these: if a size looks off in
the platform's own preview, trust the platform and update this file.

Default when the brief doesn't say: **1080 × 1350 (4:5)** for feeds and
**1080 × 1920 (9:16)** for stories.

| Platform | Placement | Size (px) | Ratio | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Instagram | Feed post / carousel | 1080 × 1350 | 4:5 | The profile grid shows a 3:4 crop, so keep key content away from the top and bottom 45 px |
| Instagram | Square post | 1080 × 1080 | 1:1 | |
| Instagram | Story / Reel cover | 1080 × 1920 | 9:16 | Safe zone below |
| Snapchat | Story / Snap ad | 1080 × 1920 | 9:16 | Keep the top 150 px and bottom 340 px free of text and logo |
| TikTok | In-feed / image ad | 1080 × 1920 | 9:16 | Right side has buttons: keep about 140 px clear on the right, bottom 400 px clear |
| X | Single image | 1600 × 900 | 16:9 | 1080 × 1080 also works; keep text central |
| LinkedIn | Feed post | 1080 × 1350 or 1200 × 1200 | 4:5 or 1:1 | |
| LinkedIn | Link image | 1200 × 627 | 1.91:1 | |
| Facebook | Feed post | 1080 × 1350 | 4:5 | |
| Facebook | Story | 1080 × 1920 | 9:16 | Safe zone below |
| WhatsApp | Status | 1080 × 1920 | 9:16 | Same safe zone as stories |
| WhatsApp | Broadcast image | 1080 × 1350 | 4:5 | Square crops in previews: keep the message central |

## Story safe zone (Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp)

On a 1080 × 1920 canvas, keep text, logo and buttons inside the middle band:
about **250 px clear at the top** (profile name, progress bar) and **340 px
clear at the bottom** (reply bar, CTA sticker). Background can bleed to the
edges.

## Margins

At least 6% of the width on every side, about 64 px on a 1080 px canvas, on
top of any safe zone.
