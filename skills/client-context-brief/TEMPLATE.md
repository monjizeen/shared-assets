## Question bank

Ask only what the material didn't answer. Most important first, at most 10.

1. Who is the one customer you most want to reach? (age, city, what they want)
2. Arabic first, English first, or both? If both, which leads?
3. Which Arabic style: formal (فصحى) or Saudi/Gulf everyday?
4. Can you send your logo files and colour codes? (or: "We found these on your site, are they right?")
5. Which fonts do you use? (or: "Shall we pick one for you?")
6. Show us 2–3 posts or designs you liked, yours or anyone's. What do you like about them?
7. Anything we must never do or say?
8. Any line that must appear on every piece? (licence number, VAT note, phone, website)
9. What do you always check before you approve?
10. Who approves, and how fast do they usually reply?
